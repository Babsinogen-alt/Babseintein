Obstetric dating

Dating & EDD

CRL dating is kept explicitly separate from MSD. Enter the scan date and CRL in millimetres. The published CRL equation returns gestational age in days; Babsinogen converts days to weeks + days before displaying GA and EDD.

Pregnancy timeline

Conception, fertile window & trimester milestones

Calendar estimates are probabilistic. Irregular cycles make ovulation and “safe period” estimates less reliable.

Developmental terminology

This application uses embryo through 10+6 gestational weeks and fetus from 11+0 gestational weeks.
