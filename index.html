Growth surveillance & longitudinal analysis

Serial assessment is preferred. v2.2 includes an embedded INTERGROWTH-21st EFW reference for completed weeks 22–40, with linear interpolation between weekly values. Use the reference appropriate to your local protocol when different.

A centile is a size descriptor, not a diagnosis. Consider FGR using the established gestational age, EFW/AC centiles, serial change, Doppler, fluid and clinical context. Do not use a single scan to infer growth velocity.

Obstetric Doppler

UA Doppler is the principal Doppler surveillance tool in established fetal growth restriction. MCA, CPR, uterine artery and ductus venosus parameters require context and appropriate reference standards.

Technique reminders

Kidney

Record side, longitudinal length, morphology, cortical echogenicity and corticomedullary differentiation.

Bladder

Wall thickness is strongly dependent on bladder distension. Record the state of distension when relevant.

Prostate

Use three orthogonal dimensions for volume when prostate size is the clinical question; intravesical protrusion is supportive, not diagnostic by itself.

Vessels

Use a standardized segment and plane and the appropriate vascular reference/guideline.

IOTA Simple Rules

Five B-features and five M-features. If only B features are present, the lesion is classified benign; only M features, malignant; both or neither, inconclusive.

Bosniak 2019

Bosniak v2019 is a CT/MRI-centered system for cystic renal masses; do not force a class from incomplete ultrasound characterization.

Breast ultrasound guide

Use lesion shape, orientation, margins, echogenicity, posterior features, vascularity, calcifications, architectural distortion and complete BI-RADS® assessment.

Thyroid ultrasound guide

Assess the entire gland, each nodule and cervical lymph nodes; score nodules with ACR TI-RADS® where appropriate.

Scrotal ultrasound guide

Assess testes, epididymides, vascularity, tunica/scrotal wall and relevant inguinoscrotal region. Acute torsion is time-critical.

Reference & Clinical-use framework v2.3
Dating: First-trimester CRL dating is preferred when appropriate. Mean sac diameter is not used to assign an EDD. When an ART-derived gestational age is available, use the ART-derived dating basis.
Growth: EFW is an estimate with inherent measurement/model error. v2.3 uses the Hadlock four-parameter EFW equation and an INTERGROWTH-21st EFW reference framework within its supported gestational-age range. Percentiles describe size relative to the selected reference; they do not by themselves establish fetal growth restriction.
Doppler: UA, MCA, CPR, uterine-artery and ductus-venosus measurements are recorded with reference/context rather than a universal raw-value normal cutoff. Absent/reversed end-diastolic flow is explicitly highlighted for clinical review.
